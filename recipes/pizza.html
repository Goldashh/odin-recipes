Cheese Pizza Recipe

Description

Pizza is one of the most delicious foods in the world.There is nothing like it.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Cheese
  • Tomato sauce

Steps

  1. First, take your dough and shape it like a circle
  2. Then, pour sauce and spread it on the dough
  3. Put cheese and bake at 180 degrees for 30 minutes
