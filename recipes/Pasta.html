Description

Alfredo Pasta is a very famous dish that originated in Italy.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Cream
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Pasta
  • Chicken
  • Mushrooms

Steps

  1. First, pour water in a pot and let it boil
  2. Next, in a pan, throw in a chunk of butter and fry chicken and mushrooms
  3. Add milk, cream and cheese and throw your pasta in the boiled water
  4. Mix the two and serve with some grated cheese
